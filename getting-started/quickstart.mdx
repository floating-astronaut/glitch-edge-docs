This is the path from zero to a working paper strategy. Total time: about five minutes,
assuming you already have a Cloudbet account.

## 1. Create your Glitch Edge account

Go to <Tooltip tip="The app lives here. Marketing + pricing at the root.">[edge.glitchexecutor.com/app/](https://edge.glitchexecutor.com/app/)</Tooltip>
and click **Create account**. Either:

- Email + password (8+ chars)
- Continue with Google

Both sign-in methods can link to the same account by email.

## 2. Get a Cloudbet API key

<Note>
  Glitch Edge places bets through your Cloudbet account — funds stay there.
  We never custody money.
</Note>

1. Sign in to Cloudbet (or [create an account via our affiliate link](https://www.cloudbet.com/en/join?af_token=01f079547c54b937449de8ab33567f55) — costs you nothing, keeps the Free tier free)
2. Deposit at least **€10** to unlock API access
3. In your Cloudbet account page, generate an **API key** and copy it

## 3. Connect the key

In Glitch Edge, navigate to **Cloudbet Key** in the sidebar and click **Add key**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Paste the key">
    The full JWT. We validate it against Cloudbet **before** storing it — invalid
    keys are rejected outright; nothing reaches the database.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Label it">
    Default works. The label is for your own bookkeeping if you ever have multiple
    keys.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    On success the key card shows the fingerprint (first 3 + last 4 chars), an
    "active" badge, and "Last verified just now".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 4. Define your first strategy

Click **Strategies** → **New strategy**. Three sections:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Match">
    Sport + market + optional league.

    Example: `basketball / moneyline / NBA`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Filter">
    Odds range + side.

    Example: `min 1.5, max 3.0, BACK` — only fires on bets priced between 1.5 and 3.0
    on the back side of the market.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sizing">
    Method + value.

    Example: `fixed_fraction / 0.02` — stakes 2% of `stake_cap` per bet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

You'll also set:

- **Daily bankroll cap** — hard limit on rolling-24h stake. Worker refuses to exceed.
- **Per-bet stake cap** — max stake on any single bet.

New strategies start **paused + paper-only**. You explicitly flip both flags when ready.

## 5. Resume the strategy

On the strategy card, click **Resume**. The worker will pick it up on the next tick
(within 60 seconds). Watch **Bets** in the sidebar — paper bets will appear as the
worker finds qualifying events.
